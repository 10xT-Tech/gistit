Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Applies to the Gist It app for iPhone

In short

  • No account, no sign-up. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • What you type, say or photograph is sent only to produce your answer. It is not stored on our servers and not used for advertising.
  • Your history, saved phrases and session recordings stay on your iPhone.
  • Analytics count which features are used. They never include your content.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifier. We never sell data.

Who we are

Gist It is made by 10XT LIMITED (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Questions or requests: info@10xt.tech.

What happens to what you enter

Gist It explains, translates and suggests phrases with AI services. To do that, the content of a request has to leave your phone. This is the only reason it is sent, and only when you ask for something.

Text and photos → Anthropic (Claude)

Text you type, paste or share into the app, and photos you choose in See it, are sent through our server to Anthropic, whose Claude models produce the answer. Our server passes the request on and returns the answer; it does not store or log the content. Anthropic processes it under its commercial API terms and does not use it to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Live speech (Online mode) → Soniox

When you turn on Online translation, audio from the microphone is streamed directly from your phone to Soniox, which turns speech into text and translates it in real time. Our server only issues the app a short-lived access key for each session; the audio never passes through it. Soniox processes real-time audio transiently: it doesn't store the audio or transcripts and doesn't use them to train its models. See Soniox's privacy policy.

Voice input → Apple

When you dictate with the microphone button, recognition is done by Apple's speech recognition service, under Apple's privacy policy. Reading phrases aloud happens on your device.

The microphone and photo library are used only after you allow them in iOS, and only when you start a recording or pick a photo. You can change these permissions any time in iOS Settings.

What stays on your iPhone

Your request history (including the answers), your study list, the audio recordings of Online sessions, a cache of recent translations and your settings are stored only on your device. We don't receive them. They are included in your iPhone backups like other app data. You can delete items in the app (swipe to delete in History, Clean up in Study, Clear cache in Settings), and deleting the app removes all of them. A study list you export goes only where you choose to send the file.

What we collect to run and improve the app

Usage analytics
Named events such as “translation completed” or “phrase saved”, plus app sessions, app version, device model, iOS version, language and an app-specific device identifier. Approximate location (country, city) may be derived from the IP address. Processed by Amplitude in its EU data center. Never includes your text, speech or photos.
Crash reports
When the app crashes or freezes: technical details of the error, app version, device model and iOS version. Processed by Sentry (United States). We configure it to collect no personal information, no screenshots and none of your content.
Device check
The app proves to our server that it is genuine Gist It on a real iPhone, using Apple's App Attest. Our server keeps an anonymous key for your installation and request counters (for rate limits and the free plan's allowance). They expire automatically.
Subscription
Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. To unlock Gist It Pro, the app sends our server Apple's signed proof of your subscription, which includes an anonymous transaction ID.
Server logs
Our server (Amazon Web Services, London) keeps technical error logs for 30 days. They don't contain the content of your requests.

We use this information to provide the app's features, keep the service secure and prevent abuse, enforce the free plan, fix crashes and understand which features are worth improving. Under UK and EU data protection law, our legal basis is performing the service you asked for and our legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable app.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads and don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA), so there is no tracking request.
  • We don't sell or rent personal data, and don't share it with data brokers.
  • We don't build profiles of you, and analytics are not linked to your identity.

Service providers and transfers

We share data only with the providers named above (Anthropic, Soniox, Apple, Amplitude, Sentry and Amazon Web Services), each only for its stated purpose and under its own data protection terms. Some of them process data outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States. In that case the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised under UK and EU law, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, or to receive it in a portable format. Email info@10xt.tech and we will reply within one month. Because we have no accounts, we hold very little that identifies you; most of your data lives on your iPhone, where you control it directly. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Gist It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

This website

The Gist It website (gistit.10xt.tech) uses Google Analytics to count visits. This page doesn't load it.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
